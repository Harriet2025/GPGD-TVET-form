About Us

Graduates and Professionals of Ga-Dangme Network

A community built on shared identity, shared knowledge, and shared advancement, now extending that strength into the skilled trades.

Who We Are

A network of graduates and professionals, united in purpose

GPGD Network brings together graduates and working professionals of Ga-Dangme heritage into a single, supportive network. Members mentor one another, share opportunities, and pool their collective expertise to lift up the wider community, from apprentices just starting out to master tradespeople shaping their industries.

That spirit of collective strength is captured in the network's own motto, carried on its emblem in the Ga language:

“Kake peemi mi, he wami ngɛ; ekome feemɔ mli, he walɛ yɔɔ.” A motto in the Ga language affirming that there is strength in unity and collective effort.

The Artisans Training Programme, run through GPGD Consultancy, is a direct expression of that motto, a practical, structured way for the network to invest in its members' futures.

Our Mission

To equip artisans, apprentices, and master trainers within and around the GPGD community with practical trade skills valued by employers and clients, through structured, accessible workshop training.

Our Vision

A future where every member of the Ga-Dangme graduate and professional community has the trade skills and credentials to compete confidently in the modern workforce.

The Programme

GPGD Network Artisans Training Programme

Workshop training built on hands on practice, turning trade experience into skill that employers and customers recognize.

The programme is designed for two groups: Master Trainers, experienced tradespeople sharpening or certifying their existing skills, and Apprentice Trainees learning under a master toward a recognized trade. Whatever your work type, whether Auto Mechanic, Electricals, Electronics, Plumbing, Masonry, Carpentry, or another trade, every participant follows a structured registration and training path so certification reflects real, demonstrated competence.

What We Aim To Achieve

Programme Objectives

01

Build Skills Employers Want

Deliver practical, applied workshop training that mirrors real jobsite tasks in every trade.

02

Provide Recognized Certification

Award a credible, structured certification that artisans can present with confidence to clients and employers.

03

Support Master Trainers and Apprentices

Recognize the relationship between master tradespeople and their apprentices, and formalize it with real training records.

04

Strengthen the GPGD Community

Turn certification into connection, building a growing network of skilled, mutually supportive artisans.

05

Support Career Transitions

Give job seekers and career changers a clear, structured route into meaningful trade work, or their own business.

06

Promote Workshop Safety

Build safe, professional habits around tools, equipment, and jobsite conditions, for artisans of every ability.
