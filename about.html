About Us

Graduates and Professionals of Ga-Dangme Network

A community built on shared identity, shared knowledge, and shared advancement, now extending that strength into technology.

Who We Are

A network of graduates and professionals, united in purpose

GPGD brings together graduates and working professionals of Ga-Dangme heritage into a single, supportive network. Members mentor one another, share opportunities, and pool their collective expertise to lift up the wider community, from students just starting out to seasoned professionals shaping their industries.

That spirit of collective strength is captured in the network's own motto, carried on its emblem in the Ga language:

“Kake peemi mi, he wami ngɛ; ekome feemɔ mli, he walɛ yɔɔ.” A motto in the Ga language affirming that there is strength in unity and collective effort.

The GPGD IT Professional Certification Programme is a direct expression of that motto, a practical, structured way for the network to invest in its members' futures.

Our Mission

To equip students, professionals, and job seekers within and around the GPGD community with practical IT skills valued by employers, through a structured, accessible certification pathway.

Our Vision

A future where every member of the Ga-Dangme graduate and professional community has the digital skills and credentials to compete confidently in the modern workforce.

The Programme

GPGD IT Professional Certification Programme

A pathway built on practice across five levels, turning interest in technology into skill that employers recognize.

The programme is designed for three groups: students building a foundation before entering the workforce, IT professionals sharpening or broadening their existing skills, and job seekers who need a recognized credential to open new opportunities. Regardless of starting point, every participant follows the same structured path through five levels, each combining instruction with practical assessment: quizzes, projects, and exams, so certification reflects real, demonstrated competence.

Level 2 Core Skills
Level 3 Applied Practice
Level 4 Specialization
Level 5 Professional Certification
