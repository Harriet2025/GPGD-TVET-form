Who We Are

Graduates and Professionals of Ga-Dangme Network

GPGD Network brings together graduates and professionals of Ga-Dangme heritage to share knowledge, support one another's advancement, and invest in the next generation. The Artisans Training Programme, run through GPGD Consultancy, is one expression of that mission, turning community strength into practical, marketable trade skills.
