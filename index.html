Who We Are

Graduates and Professionals of Ga-Dangme Network

GPGD is a network that brings together graduates and professionals of Ga-Dangme heritage to share knowledge, support one another's advancement, and invest in the next generation. The IT Professional Certification Programme is one expression of that mission, turning community strength into practical, marketable technology skills.
